  <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>

  <p><strong>Effective Date:</strong> [Insert Date]</p>


  <p>Shop With BF respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Shop With BF iOS application.</p>


  <h2>1. What Shop With BF Does</h2>

  <p>Shop With BF lets users browse online shopping websites together in real time. A shopper can invite followers into a live shopping session where followers may view synchronized browsing, listen to live audio, and send voice notes.</p>


  <h2>2. Information We Collect</h2>

  <p>We collect only information needed to provide the app’s core features. This may include your phone number, full name, profile photo, Firebase user identifier, Apple subscription entitlement status, invite sessions, selected invitee phone numbers, synchronized URL and scroll state, and temporary voice-note data.</p>


  <h2>3. Information We Do Not Sell</h2>

  <p>We do not sell, rent, or share users’ personal information or app data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. We do not track users across other apps or websites or build behavioral advertising profiles.</p>


  <h2>4. Contacts</h2>

  <p>If you grant Contacts access, we use selected phone numbers to help send invitations and match invited users to active sessions. Contacts are not used for unrelated marketing.</p>


  <h2>5. Third-Party Shopping Websites</h2>

  <p>Shop With BF displays third-party shopping websites inside the app. We do not own or control those websites. Your use of those websites is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.</p>


  <h2>6. No Proxy or Interception</h2>

  <p>Shop With BF is not a proxy, network intermediary, or man-in-the-middle service. We do not intercept your shopping website credentials, payment card details, checkout details, or private communications with third-party shopping websites.</p>


  <h2>7. Affiliate Links</h2>

  <p>Some shop links may be affiliate links. Shop With BF may receive credit or commission from qualifying purchases. This does not change the price you pay unless clearly stated by the third-party website.</p>


  <h2>8. Voice Features</h2>

  <p>The app may support live audio and voice notes during shopping sessions. Voice notes are used for real-time session communication and are intended to be temporary and session-based where possible.</p>


  <h2>9. Data Storage</h2>

  <p>We use Firebase services for authentication, database storage, and file storage. We may also store limited data locally on your device, such as cached profile images, to improve performance.</p>


  <h2>10. Account Deletion</h2>

  <p>You may initiate account deletion from inside the app. When you close your account, we will delete your account and associated app data that we are not required to keep. If you have an active Apple subscription, closing your Shop With BF account does not cancel subscription renewal. You must manage or cancel your Apple subscription through the App Store.</p>


  <h2>11. Security</h2>

  <p>We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.</p>


  <h2>12. Children</h2>

  <p>Shop With BF is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.</p>


  <h2>13. Changes</h2>

  <p>We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Continued use of the app after updates means you accept the updated policy.</p>
